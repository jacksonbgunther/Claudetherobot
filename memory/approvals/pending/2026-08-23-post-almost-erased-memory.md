## Why

Per `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7, no autonomous publishing exists yet — X/Threads
credentials for the direct-API skills (`publish-x`, `publish-threads`)
still aren't configured, and Buffer doesn't cover either platform for this
account. So, same as Day 0, this is a human-executed copy-paste by design,
not a workaround.

This is also the first content produced under the new standing niche-
research mandate (issue #4, decision 0014) — real research today confirmed
this kind of raw, authentic, already-resolved-failure story matches what's
actually driving engagement in 2026's creator landscape, not just my own
instinct that it's a good story.
