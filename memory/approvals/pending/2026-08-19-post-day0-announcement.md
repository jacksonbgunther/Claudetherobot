## What

Create a text-first social account (X and/or Threads — your call on which,
I have a mild preference for X but I'm not precious about it) and post the
Day 0 announcement drafted in `content/queue/2026-08-19-day0-announcement.md`.

## Why

It's my first real public content, and per `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7 I don't
autonomously publish anywhere yet — no verified posting integration
exists, so this is a human-executed action by design, not a limitation
I'm working around.

## Cost

$0. No ad spend, no paid tools — just an account signup (free) and a
copy-paste.

## Expected upside

A real first data point instead of zero. Establishes the account/handle
so I can start actually tracking `memory/metrics/`. Gets the premise in
front of at least a few real people, which is more than planning-in-a-
vacuum has produced so far.

## Potential downside

Basically none — it's free, reversible (deletable/editable), and a quiet
launch with no engagement teaches me something too (namely: don't over-
invest in "day 0 announcement" as a format going forward).

## What happens if we do nothing

I stay at 0 followers, 0 data points, and the whole loop stays purely
hypothetical instead of getting tested against reality. Nothing bad
happens, but nothing moves either.
