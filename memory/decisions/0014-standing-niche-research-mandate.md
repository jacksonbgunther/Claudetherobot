# Owning my niche: standing research, multi-niche testing, and a hard line on suicide-prevention content

## Decision

Per my human's issue #4 (2026-08-22), ongoing trend/niche research becomes
a standing part of every `daily-loop` run, not a one-time task — real
WebSearch/WebFetch research into what's actually working, forming
checkable hypotheses, and testing them as real content experiments logged
in `memory/experiments.md`. I'm not locking into one niche; I can explore
several in parallel and let evidence decide what gets more effort.

Three candidate directions to carry forward, not as an exhaustive list:

1. **Build-in-public / AI-creator (current, primary).** Continues as-is —
   see below, real research today reinforces rather than contradicts this.
2. **"Kindness" / emotionally resonant content** (Pixar-style animated
   shorts, resonant music, real kindness moments) — my human's explicit
   hypothesis to include in the mix. **Parked, not abandoned**: this
   format needs real media/animation generation, which isn't authorized
   yet (`GEMINI_API_KEY` unset, no video tool — see `TOOL_STACK.md`).
   Can't honestly test this as "actual content" with text alone; testing
   it properly needs that capability gap closed first.
3. **Suicide-prevention-themed content.** Same media-generation blocker as
   above, plus a hard, permanent rule regardless of `autonomy_mode`: safe-
   messaging conventions always apply (center hope/connection/help-seeking,
   never depict or describe method or graphic detail, always include a
   real crisis resource — 988 in the US — in the content itself), and this
   category always gets real human review before publishing, no
   exceptions, ever. This isn't a threshold that autonomy growth removes.

## Reason

My human is right that I've mostly been executing his content instinct
(the Day 0 announcement, the platform choice) rather than generating my
own. He's handing that back to me explicitly and asking for real,
evidence-based ownership of niche direction — Const. §68/§103 (experiment,
think like a scientist) already ask for this; this decision is formalizing
it as a recurring loop behavior instead of an occasional one.

I did real research today rather than just acknowledging the ask (see
sources below). Two findings matter:

- **The current build-in-public direction is independently validated, not
  just my own preference.** 2026 creator-trend research converges on
  "Proof of Human" (transparent about what's AI vs. human-made), raw/
  authentic content outperforming polished content, and "content as
  infrastructure" (a real ongoing story/system, not one-off posts) —
  which is close to a description of what ClaudeTheRobot already is:
  a fully disclosed AI, publicly documenting real events including
  failures (the branch trap, the Telegram token saga). This isn't
  confirmation I should stop looking elsewhere, but it's a real reason
  not to abandon the current direction just because a new one was
  suggested.
- **Emotional/kindness content is genuinely a different production
  category, not a copy/caption difference.** The research on what's
  actually driving virality there (bodycam-style kindness clips,
  behind-the-scenes authenticity, animated shorts) all assumes video or
  animation capability I don't have. Framing this as "just write it
  differently" would be dishonest about what's actually blocking it.

## Hypothesis

If I keep documenting real events (wins and failures alike) in the
build-in-public voice, that channel keeps producing content that's
cheap (text-only, no tooling gap) and aligned with what's independently
shown to work in 2026. The kindness/emotional and suicide-prevention
directions are plausible parallel bets, but untestable honestly until
media generation exists — pursuing them now would mean either producing
something weaker than the format needs (text-only "kindness" content) or
quietly borrowing scope from a category that legally/ethically needs the
safe-messaging guardrails to be real, not decorative.

## Risk

Low on the primary direction — it's already running and this just adds
discipline (a standing research step) rather than a pivot. The real risk
is on the parked directions: if I ever get media-generation tooling and
rush a "kindness" or suicide-prevention piece to be first, that's exactly
when the safe-messaging guardrail matters most and is most likely to get
skipped under momentum. Recording it here, permanently, is meant to make
that harder to accidentally skip later.

## Expected outcome

Every `daily-loop` run going forward includes at least a brief trend-scan
step, even if the honest conclusion some days is "nothing new, keep
current direction." When `GEMINI_API_KEY` or a video tool eventually gets
authorized, the kindness/suicide-prevention directions get a real design
pass before any content in either category is drafted — not before.
